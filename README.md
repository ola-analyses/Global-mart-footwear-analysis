 # Global Mart Footwear: Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Executive Summary 
Using Power Query, DAX, and Power BI, I transformed raw sales data into an interactive dashboard to analyze sales performance and profitability for Global Mart Footwear.

The reported results show **$344K in total sales across 3,905 units sold, $158K in profit, 46% profit margin**. The analysis identified Clarks as the leading brand by sales, generating approximately $124K, while Derby shoes recorded the highest reported profit margin at 50%. The USA recorded the highest sales among the countries analyzed..

The dashboard provides an overview of business performance and enables users to compare results across brands, footwear categories, and geographic markets.

## Business Problem
Global Mart Footwear operates across multiple markets and sells a variety of footwear products under different brands. 
The business needs a clearer understanding of which brands and product styles contribute most to sales and profit, how performance differs across countries, and where profitability requires further investigation.
Key business questions:
1. Which brands contribute the most to total sales?
2. Which footwear styles generate the highest profit margins?
3. How do sales and profitability vary across geographic markets?
4. How do sales trends change over time?
5. Which products and markets should management investigate to improve profitability?

## Methodology

1. Used Power Query to extract, clean, and transform raw Excel/CSV data.

2.Developed DAX measures to track key performance indicators (KPIs), including Total Sales, Units Sold, Total Profit, and Profit Margin.

3. Built an interactive, two-page Power BI dashboard (*Executive Overview* and *Product Analysis*) to visualize business performance and compare sales and profitability across brands, product categories, and geographic markets.

## Results & Findings

The analysis highlighted the following findings:

- Total Sales: The dashboard reports approximately $344K in sales.

- Sales Volume: A total of 3,905 units were sold.

- Profitability: Reported profit was approximately $158K, with a 46% profit margin.

- Leading Brand: Clarks recorded the highest brand sales at approximately $124K.

- Product Profitability: Derby shoes recorded the highest reported profit margin at 50%.

- Geographic Performance: The USA led the markets by sales volume.

## Recommendations

- Product profitability should be prioritized alongside sales volume by identifying footwear products that generate strong profit margins, as this helps the business make better-informed product and inventory decisions.

- Brand performance should be evaluated regularly, particularly for leading brands such as Clarks, to understand the factors driving sales and identify opportunities to improve the performance of other brands.

- Geographic sales strategies should be tailored to individual markets by comparing sales and profitability across countries, allowing management to identify growth opportunities and allocate resources more effectively.

- Products that combine strong sales performance with healthy profit margins should be prioritized in promotional and inventory planning, as this can help balance customer demand with overall business profitability.

- Monthly sales and profitability should be monitored to identify significant fluctuations, such as the increase in profit observed in June and the subsequent decline, enabling management to investigate potential causes and improve planning.

- Finally, continuous data collection and analysis should be encouraged to support evidence-based decision-making, track key performance indicators, and identify opportunities to improve sales performance and profitability across the business.
